2. Item 1 — the labels existed; the ceiling is behavioural, not calibration

The report called this "the most blocking absence." It was a WIRING gap, not a data gap. Reading gold_sets verbatim — "For each query, the SET of train row indices whose trace equals the query trace" — shows per-candidate correctness was always derivable: candidate column j answers query q iff j ∈ gold[q]. I wired it, labelled 11,340 pairs, and measured both veto variants.

What the measurement says, stated plainly. The labels are correct and non-degenerate. The controls behave: shuffled labels collapse separation to +0.00058 (|sep| p95 0.04603). But every arm sits at chance by the threshold-free reading, and the reason is structural — the trace-match label is not encoded in spec-wave geometry. No threshold, and no refit, can earn +0.7000 on this bank. That is a measured ceiling, recorded as such. The bar stays 0.7000 and the dark port stays mechanically disconnected.

The decay is the result. A store-level accumulator is monotone by construction — the union of verified pairs can only grow. An argmax accuracy is not. ARM R rises from 0.1111 to a peak of 0.1759, then falls back to 0.1667. More experience did not buy more competence past a point. The store-level ratchet measured earlier (CUM 138 vs 12, margin 11.5) was a genuine prerequisite; it does not compose into readout competence at this scale.

The pre-registered rule was reused, not invented: bar = max(unigram_floor, shuffled-arm held acc) + 0.05 = 0.3188, frozen in exp_m4_readout_capacity.py before its own run. Its second branch fires — the frozen wave carries insufficient information, so a readout fix cannot pass and learnable ingress is a prerequisite. This matches the in-tree M4 result independently: held-out token accuracy 0.0 against a 0.1171 floor.

Finding, stated as a methodological result and not as a bar change. The truncation preserved filtration scale (median 15.127 Å both ways, relative difference 0.0% / 0.5%). But the extremum ratio degraded 74% while the mean ratio stayed flat. The cause: min_cross is a min-order-statistic — it shrinks as the cross set grows from 100 to 1,160 pairs. A bar placed on max_within / min_cross is therefore a function of pair count, not a fixed property of the data. The bar stays 0.8000 and stays FAIL. Redesigning that bar is an operator decision, so it is escalated, not taken.

  • A wave-family mismatch. I applied eps_hard = 0.35 from the entry-unimodular complex family to a real bank whose empirical chance coherence is 12.3× the closed form. Everything fired. Both readings were recorded; the threshold-free AUC governs.
  • A no-op arm (prior turn, retained). ARM B's scale normalisation on Betti curves was an exact duplicate of ARM A — a curve is already scale-parameterised by the ε grid. Scale normalisation bites only on the diagram, which ARM C uses.
  • An assumed semantic. My first ratchet test asserted "I" and "II" are both identity. Measured: apply_op("1234","I") = "2345" — I is increment. The real collision is C being idempotent (36 collision groups measured).
