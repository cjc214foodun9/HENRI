4. The one deep result: G-H2b root cause

The pathway-alignment residual E_p does not decay to zero under the shipped dynamics. The cause is not weight decay, not a dead-unit imbalance, and not an algebraic sign error. It is the ābar condition — the backward activation chain ā^l = relu(W̄^l ā^{l+1}) does not reconstruct the value the downstream block needs.

4.1 What the source paper actually says

Extracted verbatim from the 15-page appendix (41,476 characters) I retrieved myself:

  • “identity backward nonlinearity (D_d = I)” — benchmark runs use D_d = I; this module uses relu. That is a deviation, now disclosed in every probe.
  • “V⁰ and W̄⁰ are unused by the model yet enter E⁰_p, and a fuller treatment is left to future work.”
  • “A theory of when and why Ep grows or decreases remains to be developed.” — E_p is an open problem in the source paper.
  • Hyperparameters: ηW = 1.0, ηW̄ = 0.8, ηV = ηV̄ = 0.8, decay 1e-3 on W/W̄ and 0.01 on V/V̄.

4.2 Why four repairs failed in kind

The first three solved for W̄ at a single time point from a rank-deficient V̄. Solving is a contraction: every arm reached its target by killing the stream, which is why each receipt prints a non-collapse column (|V̄|, |ā|) beside the target column. 6. What remains for engineering

  1. Settle E_p at depth. Extend the identity with the V·dT and dV·dT terms, or reduce the layer-2 step size. One probe; discriminates derivation error from a genuine large-step regime.
  2. Re-examine ηW̄ = 0.8 as an instability, not a defect. The paper runs width 200, CIFAR-10, 50 epochs, small-MLP regime. This tree runs width 32–64 with drive_scale="sqrt_d". Record the divergence; do not retune the rate to pass.
  3. Generate the candidate bank. This is the single highest-leverage unblock: it gates G-SJ3a, G-SJ3b, and every downstream selector claim. Dedup containment was measured at 0.1367, which makes a 0.1500 bar unreachable on the dedup bank — bank identity decides the verdict.
  4. A cross-fold statistic that survives conformational breathing. The lifetime filter is falsified; the honest baseline over 120 cross pairs is 7.5000, not the 1.4444 I first reported from one pair.
  5. Veto attribution redesign. The current veto is anti-selective at its own maximum. The dark port stays blocked until separation ≥ +0.7000 is genuinely earned.
  6. The NDL causal-emergence ratchet measurement. The wiring is verified and byte-identical on the legacy path; the ratchet itself is unmeasured.
  7. Blackwell deployment on real CUDA. Nothing here clears a CUDA gate. The whole performance surface is still ahead.

7. Method: why the failures count as output

Six defect classes recurred, and naming them is itself a result:

  • A gate may assert only what its fixture can carry — a 3σ bar on a 2-row fixture; σ taken from an observed p of 0 or 1.
  • Measure the quantity the source defines, at the layer and functional form the source reports — wrong mask, disclaimed layer, ratio read as absolute. Three of my own published numbers died here.
  • A strictly monotone transform cannot split a tie — a temperature cannot fix a plateau; argmax exp(s/κ) == argmax s exactly.
  • A constant-across-candidates penalty is a strict no-op — the “new” objective is the old ranker in costume.
  • No gate may sit downstream of an operator that mixes the candidate with the bank — two such gates saturated and could not fail.
  • An unrun artifact is not a deliverable. Applied here: probe_ep_ode.py sat written-but-unrun, and I ran it before using it.
