Zone C transfer ladder — does retrieval actually transfer?

CPU only · $0 compute · live dev TimescaleDB · three seeds per rung · pre-registered kills · HEAD cbf3cd5

Answer

Yes — when and only when the family shares structure. Retrieval cut samples-to-criterion by 40% in every rung tested. Carried the same way, retrieval gave 0% benefit where families shared nothing.

The negative half is the stronger evidence. A generic warm-start would help both conditions. This does not, so the gain is retrieved content.

5. What this does not show

  • Synthetic task. Clustered diagonal-operator family. Not a benchmark, not natural language, not vision.
  • OBSERVED mechanism, not end-task capability. A pass here does not predict an R3-equivalent on real data.
  • No latency claim. The 50 µs gate still fails at 195.55 µs.
  • No AAII v4.3 score. At most 25% of the composite is locally reproducible; the harness refuses to emit a composite.
  • Egress is untested. Retrieval supplies context. Whether that context improves a generated answer is a separate, unrun experiment — and it is the binding constraint.
