3. The metric had to be amended — on evidence

A pre-registered gate for this exact question had been sitting unrun for 4 months. Its import was broken: parents[1] pointed at experiments/ instead of HENRI V2, so it raised ModuleNotFoundError and never started. One line to fix.

Once it ran, it falsified its own premise:

Armdistinct_ratio (treatment)random-wave controlVerdict
fractional_shift0.2330.592VACUOUS
phasor_bind0.1170.700VACUOUS

The random control beat the treatment. Counting distinct top-1 tokens is not evidence of semantic content — it is the known argmax-invariance defect. So A-K4 was re-specified to score content presence in the generated answer, with A0 and A2 as negative controls. The kill did not weaken; the instrument did. This amendment is recorded in the contract with its evidence.

4. Defect found by my own smoke run, fixed before the verdict

A2 was contaminated. The first version reused another item's context. With 6 chunks and k=3, every context already contains 3 of 6 facts, so A2 shared about half its evidence with A1.

Evidence: hit_A2 = 0.500 exactly — the contamination signature. After excluding the target source: hit_A2 = 0.083.

Two further real defects surfaced and were fixed:

  • Silent no-op ingest. ON CONFLICT (chunk_id) DO NOTHING made a re-ingest report tx=COMMITTED, written=1 while changing nothing in the database. The report said success; the store disagreed.
  • Retrieval-scope contamination. query_corpus filters by domain only, not by source set. An earlier probe row in the same domain entered top-3 and made build_context fail closed on a missing file.
