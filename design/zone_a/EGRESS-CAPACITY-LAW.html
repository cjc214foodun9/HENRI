Zone A Egress: the encoder was poisoning the engine

HENRI development arbitration · 2026-10-03 · operator grant: Path A and the executive instructions of HENRI-ARCH-2026-SYSTEMIC-EVALUATION-V3 (sha256 9f3cd5cd…b7bf) · all runs local CPU, $0, no GPU.

Headline. The blueprint locates the egress failure in the readout and prescribes a Hopfield lexical snap. Measurement locates it upstream, in the codec's own encode(), which hash-fills 8096 of 8192 rows from hash(text). Stripping that fill lifts a flat linear head 6× and lets a training-free centroid decode content far above chance at every scale. Two later measurements complete the picture: the manifold's atomic vocabulary size — not its cardinality — sets the egress ceiling; and typed decomposition supplies that lever.

1. Academic foundation

A single linear map W cannot compute multi-step recursion. That is true and it is not what failed here. Per-class nearest-centroid is itself a linear decision rule; when it separates classes, the representation is linearly decodable and a linear probe suffices. No capacity argument about W applies until this test passes.

Continuous Modern Hopfield retrieval performs softmax(β·s) over a codebook. Selection is argmax, which is order-preserving in β. So β sets convergence, not selection. Measured: argmax identical across β∈[0.5,1000]; accuracy steps 0.000→1.000 as β passes 5.

2. Technical decision

2.1 The located defect

zone_c_world_knowledge_codec.py lines 139–153. A phrase with F features touches F×16 slots. The rest are filled from a whole-text hash and returned as the engram. A 6-feature phrase ships 0.15% content and 99.85% text-hash noise. The fill satisfies the retrieval-side row_unit gate; egress inherited it as noise.

Fix: CompositionalTextCodec.encode_egress(), commit 1529b50. Additive. encode() is byte-unchanged; all 6 geometry gates still pass.

Control-validated. Removing the fill lifts a flat linear head 0.0586→0.3516 with every control at chance → HASH_FILL_IS_THE_SNR_DEFECT. Mechanism predicts size: 96/√96 ≈ 9.8 vs 96/√8192 ≈ 1.06; ratio 9.2 predicted, 6.0 observed.
