6. What this is not

  • Not a SOTA claim. No benchmark was run.
  • Not AAII v4.3. At most 25% of that composite is locally reproducible. The harness refuses to emit a composite score — 75% of index weight is externally graded or private.
  • Not end-task capability. The ladder used a synthetic clustered family; A-K4 used a 6-fact corpus and 12 items. Both are OBSERVED mechanism evidence on toy data. If any summary drops that qualifier, it overclaims.
  • Not latency. Unmeasured, per your instruction.
