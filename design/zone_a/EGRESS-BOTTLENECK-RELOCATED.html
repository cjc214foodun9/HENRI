Located in source, zone_c_world_knowledge_codec.py:139–153:

rows = acc.reshape(NUM_BLOCKS, BLOCK_DIM)          # [8192, 8]

    for k in np.nonzero(empty)[0]:
        x = (seed * LCG_MUL + int(k) * LCG_ADD) & 0xFFFFFFFF

Verdict HASH_FILL_IS_THE_SNR_DEFECT.

Mechanism predicts the effect size. Fill SNR ≈ 96/√8192 ≈ 1.06. No-fill SNR ≈ 96/√96 ≈ 9.8. Predicted ratio 9.2, observed 6.0. Same order — this is not a fitting artifact.
