Codec content recovery: the instrument measured its own detection floor

Question: does the S4 codec wave family carry recoverable token content? Answer: this method cannot resolve that at the codec's native scale. The instrument is valid — it detected every injected signature above 0.78% of the wave. The codec's own one-word footprint is 0.20%. That gap is the result.

Measured, not inferred.
  • Positive control is strictly monotone: 0.688 → 1.000 across six rungs.
  • Same-family content-destroyed control sits at exactly 0.500 (margin +0.0001). Valid negative.
  • Detection floor: 64 blocks = 0.78% of the wave.
  • Codec one-word footprint: 16 blocks = 0.20%.
Floor above footprint ⇒ real-wave numbers are not interpreted. Verdict INSTRUMENT_CANNOT_RESOLVE_CODEC_SCALE is a limitation of the method, not a finding about the codec.

2. Directional hint — explicitly not a result

REAL 0.642 vs SHF 0.500, and REAL's margin (+0.0081) is roughly 4× the POS-16 margin (+0.0019). That is consistent with a weak content signal at the codec's native scale. It does not clear the pre-registered 0.80 bar, and the floor rule forbids interpreting it. Recorded as a hint for a more sensitive follow-up. It is not evidence.
