HENRI development arbitration · 2026-10-03 · operator grant: Path A plus the executive instructions of HENRI-ARCH-2026-SYSTEMIC-EVALUATION-V3 (sha256 9f3cd5cd…b7bf) · every run local CPU, $0, no GPU, no store.

Headline. The blueprint locates the egress failure in the readout and prescribes a Continuous Modern Hopfield “Lexical Snap” at β* = 26.10. Five independent measurements reject that account and replace it with a working one. The failure was in the encoder, not the readout; β does not select anything; and the remaining deficit was my training procedure, not the architecture. The delivered fix is a training-free typed egress head that reaches 0.9141 on held-out templates where gradient training reached 0.4062.

1. Academic foundation

A single linear map cannot compute multi-step recursion. That is true, and it is not what failed here. Per-class nearest-centroid is itself a linear decision rule; when it separates classes the representation is linearly decodable and no architectural change is licensed. Separating “the representation is inadequate” from “my learner is inadequate” requires one corpus and several readout families under the same splits — which is what §3.3 measures.

Continuous Modern Hopfield retrieval computes softmax(β·s) over a codebook; selection is argmax, which is order-preserving in β. So β governs convergence, not identity of the chosen item. A “hyperparameter lock” on β therefore cannot be the mechanism that fixes a selection collapse.

2. Technical decision

2.1 The located defect: the encoder poisons the wave

zone_c_world_knowledge_codec.py lines 139–153. A phrase with F features touches F×16 slots. Every remaining row is filled from a whole-text hash and shipped as the engram. A 6-feature phrase therefore ships 0.15% content and 99.85% text-hash noise. The fill satisfies a retrieval-side row_unit gate; egress inherited it as noise.

2.9 Stage 6: hierarchical active inference — bounded, not validated

The blueprint's third named component (sec 1.4, Stage 6) claims the wave core lacks "a recursive active inference loop to execute step-by-step state manipulation." That claim is checkable without any learning. Given a calibrated noisy readout over the real codec waves, does Expected Free Energy (EFE) probe selection beat greedy selection? Noise per rung is calibrated so single-look argmax accuracy hits a set target.

K=6, real corpus facts (400 trials/rung, 5 rungs): G-AI-1 (info median ≤ 0.75×greedy) fires at one rung only, 0.45 — info 2.0 vs greedy 3.0 probes. At the measured operating accuracy 0.8043 and at every rung ≥ 0.55 it does not beat greedy. What held at every rung: info beats random on mean probes (1.45/1.67 … 4.57/7.58). K=6 result is DERIVED: it tests the decision rule, not hardware.

K=512 (the blueprint's own bound; real waves, synthetic labels, so also DERIVED): verdict INSTRUMENT_CANNOT_RESOLVE_EFE. The control could not lose at any rung, so no policy comparison is meaningful. Cause measured, not assumed (D39): at N_MC=4 the entropy estimate has sd 0.3028 and the spread across 24 candidates is 1.1813 — larger than the mean 0.7176, so the policy cannot rank candidates.

Common random numbers did not repair it (SM6_CRN=1, N_MC=32, 816 s, RC=0): the median stayed pinned at 2.0 for all three policies at both rungs. A pinned median is a property of the statistic, not of the ranking variance — so the named N_MC=64/128 escalation is cancelled as the repair. Monte Carlo cannot move a median that every policy already attains at step 2. At K=6 the same statistic did separate, so the limitation belongs to the metric and is K-dependent. Raising CRITERION to re-open headroom would move a pre-registered gate to rescue a null; that is refused, not done.

My own proposed repair also failed. The instrument already printed a different statistic — per-policy acc@1 — so testing it needed power, not a new instrument. At 800 trials/rung the info−greedy difference reverses sign between runs (−0.0567 at 300 trials, +0.0312 at 800, rung 0.55; SE ≈ 0.025). Three statistics now fail at K=512: median steps (pinned at 2.0 — no dynamic range), mean steps (never separates), acc@1 (sign-unstable). No K=512 policy ranking is established, and that includes the two repairs recommended by external review — the N_MC escalation and acc@1 power — each refuted by run, not by argument.
