Why the asymmetry — it is parameter count, not luck

FACTORIZED holds 524,352 parameters against 64 support pairs — it can memorize, and it does (loss → 0.0000), then generalizes at chance. DIAGONAL holds 4,096 — it cannot memorize (loss floors at 0.238), and only it generalizes. Exact roll + value rotation is elementwise, so its target is full-rank diagonal with a flat spectrum; a rank-64 truncation keeps ~3% of the energy. The spec's rank-256 transition is structurally wrong for elementwise ARC-style transforms.
