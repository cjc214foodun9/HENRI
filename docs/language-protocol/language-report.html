Operational

Short, active instructions. Soft targets: 20 words per instruction, 25 per description, three words per noun group.

One task per sentence. State the actor, evidence class, risk, and next gate.

Formal

Code, equations, schema keys, units, identifiers, hashes, paths, and source quotes retain their exact form.

No simplification of mathematics to fit a style checker.

Visual / HITL

Use editable Draw.io diagrams and accessible HTML for substantive status, decisions, and explanations.

Layout, graph richness, labels, and explanatory prose remain unrestricted. Safety and evidence controls remain active.

Safety remains readable.

WARNING marks injury or death risk. CAUTION marks damage to equipment, state, or data. An urgent warning never waits for a diagram render.

Text alternative: fixed policy → declared track → advisory assessment → independent approval and proof → observed outcome. No edge permits language advice to authorize a command.

Adopt

  • Short sentences and clear actors.
  • Defined terms and stable meanings.
  • Separate operational and formal content.
  • Use diagrams to explain complex structures.
  • Use a lenient checker to avoid repair thrash.

Correct / reject

  • Issue 9 replaces the supplied Issue 8 pin.
  • “Approximately” is approved for estimates; “about” has another meaning.
  • WARNING is not a disk-error severity code.
  • Regex is not a complete dictionary or grammar AST.
  • A prose label does not map to native OCSF status.
  • Language constraints do not guarantee correct JSON or commands.
